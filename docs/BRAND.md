# Agent Allowance brand

Spending power for AI, with a human key.

The mark is an open ring. The gap is the limit. The dot rides the ring, which is the allowance still left. It is not a warning, and it is not a letterform with a bar through it.

## Iterations

1. A closed ring with a stem and a gold dot in the middle. At 16px it read as an exclamation point. That is a warning glyph, and it fights the product: the allowance is a limit you set, not an alarm.
2. A thin almost-complete ring with a small dot floating inside the gap. The ring looked like a spinner, and the dot disappeared at 16px. The two parts did not belong to one shape.
3. A thicker open ring, with the dot seated on the open end. The stroke is heavy enough to read at 16px. The lime dot is only on the pine tile, where it stays a small accent. This is the mark in the app.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pine | `#1B4D3E` | Primary, the hero, the app icon |
| Cream | `#F7F4EC` | Type on pine, light tile |
| Page | `#EFECE4` | Page background |
| Ink | `#1A1814` | Text on cream |
| Accent | `#C6F36B` | The dot on the pine tile only |
| Amber | `#8A4518` | Waiting |
| Red | `#8E2218` | Blocked |

Do not paint large areas with the accent. Amber and red are status, not decoration.

## Type

Inter 400, 500, 600, and 700 for the interface. Outfit 700 is only the balance and large headings. Labels are 13px, sentence case, with no tracking. The lime accent is only the ring that shows what is left.

## Voice

Plain sentences. Say what happened and what the person can do. Do not shout in labels, and do not joke with sample identifiers.
